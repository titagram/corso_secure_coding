📝 Lab Parte 3: Esercizio Pratico

Confronta email legittima vs email spoofata
✅ Email Legittima
From: mg.groupe@monaco.mc Return-Path: mg.groupe@monaco.mc Message-ID: <xyz@smtp.monaco.mc> Received: from smtp.monaco.mc (smtp.monaco.mc [real-ip]) by mx.nuovokolor.com Authentication-Results: spf=pass smtp.mailfrom=monaco.mc dkim=pass header.d=monaco.mc dmarc=pass header.from=monaco.mc
❌ Email Fraudolenta
From: mg.groupe@rnonaco.cam ← FALSO! Return-Path: noreply@rnonaco.cam Message-ID: <abc@vps123.hosting.com> Received: from vps123.hosting.com (unknown [185.x.x.x]) by mx.nuovokolor.com Authentication-Results: spf=pass smtp.mailfrom=rnonaco.cam dkim=none dmarc=none
🔍 Differenze Chiave da Identificare
1. Dominio: monaco.mc vs rnonaco.cam (m → rn, .mc → .cam)

2. Server origine: smtp.monaco.mc vs vps123.hosting.com (VPS generico!)

3. IP: IP aziendale Monaco vs IP hosting economico (185.x.x.x spesso = VPS russo/ucraino)

4. DKIM/DMARC: La legittima ha tutto configurato, la fraudolenta no
📋 Compito
L'istruttore fornirà header reali. Identifica: server origine, IP, risultati SPF/DKIM/DMARC, e determina se l'email è legittima o spoofata.
